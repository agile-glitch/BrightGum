# Stick-gum package sizes (12–18 stick, Wrigley style)

What the most common US stick-gum packs measure, to size bright's carton.

> **How these were found:** from retailer product data, trade press and a US
> military gum specification, gathered November 2026. Supplier pages couldn't be
> opened directly, so figures come from search-result extracts. Measure a real
> pack before ordering a printing die.

## The most common size: the 15-stick "Slim Pack"

Extra, Doublemint, Juicy Fruit, Big Red and Wrigley's Spearmint all use the same
15-stick Slim Pack, introduced in 2008 to replace the 17-stick pack.

| | Inches | Millimeters |
|---|---|---|
| Height | **2.99–3.00** | 76 |
| Width | **2.44** | 62 |
| Depth | **0.43** | 11 |

- **Inside:** three side-by-side stacks of five sticks, each stick running the
  long way (the 3 in side).
- **Opening:** a flip-lid paperboard carton.
- **Contents:** 15 sticks × 2.7 g = 40.5 g (1.43 oz).

**Range for 15-stick cartons:** 2.99–3.08 in tall, 2.44–2.5 in wide and
0.43–0.48 in deep.

## A single stick

The US military gum specification (A-A-20175, stick gum) sets maximums of
3.00 × 0.87 × 0.09 in (76 × 22 × 2.4 mm) and 2.6–3.2 g. bright's recipes use
2.7 g sticks, the same as Extra.

## Counter display box (10 packs)

About 5.1 × 3.2–3.5 × 2.4–2.8 in (low confidence, from listings). Cases
usually hold 12 displays of 10 packs.

## What this means for bright

| | Your mockup | Most common pack | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Height | 3.00 in | 2.99–3.00 in | Keep 3.00 in |
| Width | 2.40 in | 2.44 in | Use **2.44 in** for the production carton |
| Depth | 0.35 in | 0.43 in | Use **0.43 in** (about 11 mm) |

The front and back labels in `bright-cartons.pdf` are 3.00 × 2.40 in, sized to
paste onto an existing carton. That still works for mockups: on a 2.44 in pack
they leave a 0.02 in border on each side.

The depth is the one to recheck. Five stacked sticks (about 2 mm each) plus
wrappers and two carton walls come to about 11 mm, so 0.35 in (8.9 mm) is likely
too thin. Measure a full, unopened pack without squeezing it. For the production
carton, ask the packaging printer to design the dieline around
**3.00 × 2.44 × 0.43 in** with a flip lid, and to confirm it with a sample run.
