# bright gum: marketing plan

## The one-line pitch

> **bright is a whitening xylitol gum with a positive message inside every
> wrapper.**

Every other gum sells *fresh breath*. bright sells **a small good moment**:
you unwrap it, read something kind and smile. The logo is literally a winking
smile, and the product delivers one. Build everything on that.

**Tagline:** *Something to smile about.* It works three ways: white teeth, the
logo's smile and the wrapper message.

---

## 1. Who buys it first

Start with communities you can reach in person, where word of mouth travels.

| Audience | Why they'll care | Where to reach them | Edition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **College students** (your home base) | Chew gum constantly, share everything on social media, love a quirky flavor | Campus, dorms, rec center, student orgs, TikTok | bright (everyday) |
| **Gym-goers** | Already chew gum while lifting; respond to encouragement | Rec center, local gyms, fitness creators | bright (everyday) |
| **Churches & youth groups** | Verse-a-day is meaningful; buy in bulk for events, camps and gifts | Youth pastors, Christian bookstores, church cafés | **bright faith** |
| **Dentists & hygienists** | Xylitol is already something they recommend; they hand out gum | Local dental offices (free samples for their front desk) | either |

**Shared trait:** they all *give gum to other people*. That's built into your
commercials (the hand-off), so lean on it: **the gum is a way to share a kind
word.**

## 2. Positioning against the shelf

| | Extra / Orbit / Trident | Xylitol brands (PUR, Spry, Epic) | **bright** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main promise | Fresh breath, long-lasting flavor | Healthy, aspartame-free | **Whitening + xylitol + a message** |
| Feel | Mass-market | Health-store | **Warm, fun, shareable** |
| Reason to talk about it | None | Ingredients | **Every stick is different** |

You don't have to beat the giants on price or flavor. You win on **the one
thing nobody else does**: the message.

## 3. Pricing (starting point)

- A 15-stick carton of a mainstream brand typically sells for about **$1.50–2.00**;
  specialty xylitol gums often run **$2–3+** for a similar amount. *(Check local
  shelves; prices vary.)*
- **Suggested retail: $2.49 per 15-stick carton.** Premium, but still an impulse buy.
- **Bulk "share box"** (12 cartons) for churches, teams and offices at about $24.
- **Rule of thumb for margins:** if you sell through stores, the retailer keeps
  roughly 35–50% of the shelf price, and a distributor may take another 15–25%.
  Your cost per carton from the co-packer (gum + wrapper + carton) should stay
  around **$0.60–0.85** to leave room for that. Get real quotes before you set
  the price.

## 4. Launch plan in phases

### Phase 0: before you have product (now → samples)

- [ ] Lock the handles: **@brightgum** (or close) on TikTok, Instagram and
      YouTube, plus the domain.
- [ ] Post the **making-of story**: *"I designed my gum logo in Minecraft, then
      sketched it, then printed a mockup box."* That's great content as it is.
      Start posting now.
- [ ] Simple landing page with an **email sign-up** ("Be first to try bright").
- [ ] **Flavor vote** poll: "Would you try PICKLE gum?" The comments will do
      your marketing.

### Phase 1: campus launch (first 1–3 months with product)

- [ ] **Sampling tables** at the rec center, student union and move-in week. The
      ask: *"Take one, read your wrapper, pass one to a friend."*
- [ ] **Campus ambassadors** (5–10 students) with free cartons, a referral code,
      and a goal of posting their wrapper reactions.
- [ ] Get stocked in the **campus bookstore / convenience store**. Your story as
      a student founder is the pitch.
- [ ] Film the two commercials (see `commercials.md`) and post them.

### Phase 2: online growth (months 3–6)

- [ ] **Sell direct** on your own site (Shopify) with a **monthly subscription**
      (people chew gum every day).
- [ ] Wholesale listing on **Faire** so small shops (gift stores, Christian
      bookstores, gym front desks) can order.
- [ ] Ship free cartons to **micro-creators** (5k–50k followers) in fitness,
      college life and faith content. Ask them to disclose with #ad or #gifted.

### Phase 3: retail expansion (6–12 months)

- [ ] Use your sales numbers and social proof to pitch **regional grocery chains**
      and **convenience stores** near campuses.
- [ ] Consider a **food broker** (they pitch retailers for a commission).
- [ ] Amazon, once you can keep inventory steady.

## 5. Content ideas that fit this brand

| Idea | Format |
|---|---|
| **"Read your wrapper"**: people react on camera to their message | TikTok/Reels trend; repost the best ones |
| **#brightside**: tag a friend who needs a message today | UGC hashtag campaign |
| **The Pickle Test**: strangers try pickle gum | Street-interview video (it gets watched) |
| **Message of the day**: a daily post of one wrapper message | Instagram stories, a daily habit |
| **Founder story**: Minecraft → sketch → mockup → real gum | Series; document every step |
| **Coffee drinker's gum**: chew after your latte | Ties whitening to a daily moment |
| **Collect them all**: post your wrapper collection | Encourages repeat buying |
| **Origami wrappers** (kids'/family edition) | How-to videos |

## 6. Ideas that set bright apart

- **Submit-a-message.** Customers send in positive messages; picked ones get
  printed on a future run, credited by first name. It builds a community and
  gives you new copy for free.
- **Give-one program.** For each share box sold, donate a carton to a
  hospital, shelter or school counselor's office. It fits a kindness brand.
- **Seasonal editions.** Exam week ("You've studied enough. Breathe."),
  Christmas (bright faith verses), Mental Health Awareness Month.
- **Dentist's office starter pack.** 50 cartons at cost and a counter display.
  It earns credibility for the whitening and xylitol story.

## 7. What to measure

| Stage | Metric | Early target |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Followers, video views | First video above 10k views |
| Interest | Email sign-ups | 500 before launch |
| Trial | Samples handed out | 2,000 on campus |
| Purchase | Cartons sold per week | Track from week one |
| Loyalty | Repeat-buy and subscription rate | 25%+ of buyers reorder |
| Buzz | Customer posts with #brightside | Any is a win early on |

## 8. Marketing rules to stay legal

- Say **"whitening"** only once you have test data. Never say **"anti-cavity,"
  "prevents cavities"** or **"dentist recommended"** (see `regulations/regulations.md`).
- Don't suggest the gum improves workouts, energy or mood. The *message*
  encourages people; the gum doesn't make them stronger.
- Paid or gifted influencers must disclose (#ad, #gifted).
- Show a **dog warning** on the website product page (xylitol).
