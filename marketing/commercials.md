# bright gum: commercial scripts

Two 30-second spots built from your drafts (commercial_1 and commercial_2). Both
are built around one moment: **she reads the wrapper, and she smiles.**
That smile *is* the logo, so every spot ends on it.

Shoot vertical (9:16) for TikTok/Reels/Shorts first. Frame wide enough to crop a
16:9 version later.

---

## Spot 1: "Pass It On" (campus)

**Location:** campus walkway, green lawn and trees behind. Golden hour if possible.
**Cast:** HER (main), FRIEND.

| Time | Shot | Action | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | **Extreme close-up** | FRIEND's hand offers a stick of bright gum; HER hand takes it. Green campus blurred behind. | Ambient campus, light music starts |
| 0:03–0:07 | **Medium, 5–10 ft** | HER unwraps the stick and pops it in her mouth, mid-conversation. | FRIEND (off-screen laugh) |
| 0:07–0:10 | **Medium wide** | They say bye. HER walks off, wrapper still in hand. | "See you later!" |
| 0:10–0:13 | **Wide, camera pulls back** | HER heads for a trash can and lifts the wrapper to toss it… | Music dips |
| 0:13–0:15 | **Close-up on wrapper** | …she stops. There's writing on it. | Music pauses |
| 0:15–0:19 | **Insert, wrapper fills frame** | We read it: *"Somebody is really glad you exist."* | Silence, then a soft "huh." |
| 0:19–0:23 | **Slow push-in on her face** | She smiles. | Music returns, warmer |
| 0:23–0:27 | **Behind her, walking away** | She pockets the wrapper (doesn't throw it out) and goes on with her day. | |
| 0:27–0:30 | **End card** | bright logo on white. The face **winks** (animated). Tagline below. | VO: "bright gum. Something to smile about." |

**Detail that matters:** she *keeps* the wrapper. That's the story: the message
is worth keeping.

## Spot 2: "Lift" (rec center)

**Location:** outside the rec center, then inside the weight room.
**Cast:** HER, FRIEND.

0:00–0:19 follows Spot 1's beats (hand-off outside the rec, chew, goodbye, she
notices the wrapper), but the wrapper reads ***"You're stronger than you think."***
or ***"Finish strong."***

Then cut to one of two endings:

### Ending A: "Heavy"

| Time | Shot | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0:19–0:21 | Close-up | She reads it, smiles, and tucks the wrapper into her water-bottle sleeve. |
| 0:21–0:26 | Fast cuts, low angle | Battle ropes slamming, sweat, a heavy deadlift lockout, treadmill sprint. |
| 0:26–0:28 | Close-up | Between sets, she glances at the wrapper and grins. |
| 0:28–0:30 | End card | Logo wink. VO: "bright gum. Something to smile about." |

### Ending B: "Calm"

| Time | Shot | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0:19–0:24 | Medium | She walks into the gym relaxed, nods to someone, and starts her warm-up. |
| 0:24–0:28 | Close-up | A small smile mid-set. Confident, not strained. |
| 0:28–0:30 | End card | Logo wink. VO: "bright gum. Something to smile about." |

Shoot both and run them as an A/B test on social media to see which one people
watch to the end.

> **Keep it legal:** the ad shows her feeling encouraged, not the gum making her
> stronger. Don't add a line like "more energy" or "lift heavier." That's a
> performance claim you can't support.

## Shot list (shoot both spots in one day)

- [ ] Hand-off close-up (outside the campus walkway **and** outside the rec)
- [ ] Unwrap and chew, medium
- [ ] Goodbye and walk-off, medium-wide
- [ ] Trash-can approach and stop, wide
- [ ] **Wrapper inserts:** shoot 4–5 different messages so you can swap them in edit
- [ ] Face push-in for the smile (several takes; a natural smile beats a posed one)
- [ ] Walking away from behind
- [ ] Gym: ropes, deadlift, treadmill, the between-sets glance
- [ ] Pack beauty shots: all three flavors on the lawn and on a gym bench

**Props:** printed message wrappers (print the inside messages from `product/wrapper-messages.md` on thin paper), your carton
mockups and real gum sticks to rewrap.

## Taglines to test

- **Something to smile about.** (recommended; it ties straight to the logo)
- Unwrap something good.
- Chew on this.
- A bright side in every piece.
